Imports of moved modules become package imports (cubbyllm.*, validation.sft.*, validation.builders.*),
and a wrapped import keeps its lines and comments. The builders keep their sys.path lines, their paths
rewritten: they are scripts run by their path and reach the stand-in's modules (openrouter, the
game) through them. tools/move_slice.py learns both (path lines are dropped only in files bound for
cubbyllm/ or cubby_serve/; a module-only change is substituted in place). What stays in standin/data/:
the three builders that serve the GGUF adapters only (build_game_sft, build_profile_sft, gap_families).
