[0031] slice 10, the builders: the old names as shims

The 35 builders' old paths in standin/data/ are shims for one cycle: `python standin/data/<builder>.py`
runs the moved builder, and `import <builder>` with standin/data on the path gives the moved module
(the identity records too: standin/data/identity is validation.sft.identity, which re-exports the
runtime half from cubbyllm.speech.identity).
