[0034] The act line counted: a world's act log summed to counts that name no cell and no thing

validation/s12_act.py reads the act arm's log (BlockWorld's, private). It counts:
- plans drawn placeable that were placed on the cell the host computed, where that cell is the one the test
  expected; a lamp standing on a wrong cell is a defect, not a placement;
- markers standing anywhere else;
- plans drawn unplaceable that were refused before an order was sent.
exp_s12_scene_files adds the counts to its public log. The test pins that a wrong cell counts as neither a
placement nor a refusal.
