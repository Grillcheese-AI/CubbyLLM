[0024] The news archive: a feed manifest, an append-only poller, robots read by group

- standin/news_archive.py (new): polls the manifest's active feeds, appends each item once (key = feed + guid/link)
  to one file a day (the day first seen), headline and summary only (summary cut at 400 characters), never an
  article page. Robots are read every poll at the feed host and the publisher's site: a rule refusing CubbyLLM
  (or *) the feed path skips it, an AI-agent block the manifest has not recorded holds it. A poll log, a lock,
  --once / --every / --stats.
- standin/data/news_feeds.json (new): 15 feeds. 9 active: Government of Canada en/fr releases (Open Government
  Licence), Quebec government releases, La Presse x2, Le Devoir x2, Radio-Canada, L'actualite. 6 held: BBC, NYT,
  Guardian, NPR, Al Jazeera (their robots shut out AI agents by name), Politico (robots unreadable, HTTP 403).
  The third-party AP mirror is dropped.
- standin/robots_rules.py (new): the group-aware reader (RFC 9309). The old one gave a group's Disallow to its
  last name only, so La Presse, Le Devoir and the Guardian read as naming no AI agent. news_source.py now
  imports it (389 lines, down from 413; it was already over 300 and only shrinks here).
- standin/serve_api.py: --news-archive <dir> (default CUBBY_NEWS_ARCHIVE; off when unset) runs the hourly poll
  in a thread (Nick's choice). +7 lines on a file already over 300: the flag belongs with the other mounts.
- First poll, 2026-10-22: 283 items from the 9 active feeds, 0 duplicates.
- Tests: standin/tests/test_news_archive.py (6), test_news_source.py still green.
