**H-K7 — Décupler: the same power from twenty-five times fewer training tokens (40B for what a 1T-token model knows and does), and on facts from a million times fewer, because a fact does not have to be learned from sightings and a textbook does not have to be scraped.** Source: Nick, 2026-10-20 ("décupler [tenfold] the information without trillion of tokens and still have the same power … 10 fold could be 1,000,000 fold too, the higher the better … per example we need 40B token to train the model to be par to a 1T token model"). Inspiration: the exposure law ("Physics of Language Models 3.3": ~2 bits per parameter, ~1,000 sightings to store a fact, wording diversity needed to extract it); the data-quality result (phi: a 1.3B model on ~7B tokens of textbook-quality data matching models trained on far more web); the data-constrained scaling law (Muennighoff et al. 2023: repeating data up to ~4 epochs costs almost nothing against fresh data); RETRO and kNN-LM, Memory Layers at Scale (retrieval as knowledge). Ours: the store is H-K1's map plus the exact store, algebraic and VM-verified, not a dense index; the textbooks are written by our own loop (the VM, the walks, the scene, paraphrased by rule — no external model in Cubby's loop, OpenRouter only to build datasets); and the trunk's part is a *route* (a gate and a query, as in H-S12), not the facts. **Where the factor comes from, honestly split.** (a) **Facts — unbounded.** Most of a trillion-token corpus is its long tail: names, numbers, dates, who-did-what, each seen many times so the weights keep it. **Measured on our own trunk** (`validation/exp_k7_exposure.py`, 2026-10-20: a 1.06M-parameter `train_base` trunk, 1,000 synthetic facts in a Markov language, one pass, 10 min on the CPU): exact recall 0.00 / 0.00 / 0.50 / 0.99 at 1 / 10 / 100 / 1,000 exposures with paraphrased wordings (first object token 0.00 / 0.01 / 0.74 / 1.00), 0.00 / 0.00 / 0.30 / 0.86 verbatim, language held-out loss 6.96 → 2.67 — **gate 1 passes**: the law holds here, and wording diversity helps extraction as in its paper. A fact in the store costs one write (the exact store recalls it at 1.00 by construction, the vector map at H-K1's Hits@1): a fact scraped a million times is written once. (b) **Language — bounded by quality, not by the store.** On the same data, 25× fewer tokens costs ~2.5× the reducible loss (the Chinchilla exponent, D^−0.28); what beats the law is what the tokens are. Ours write their own textbooks: verified facts in many wordings, verified reasoning traces (BlockWorld chemistry, wiki-world walks that the VM checked), the scene's questions with answers guaranteed by the algebra — repeated up to ~4 epochs under the learning gate (H-A7 decides whether an update is CE-neutral). (c) **Structure at the input — paid in algebra, not tokens.** A village bound as a scene is learned from zero text about space (H-S12 gate 1: ≥ 90% against < 60% for the text control at the same tokens); the same mechanism carries a fact link (H-K1's phases bound into entity tokens, read mid-trunk) and a clock (H-K4). **What it brings:** the fact power of a trillion-token model from a store the size of a disk, the language of one from textbooks the loop wrote, and facts that update without a gradient step. **Advantage over conventional models:** they buy every fact with ~1,000 sightings of web text and cannot change one without training again; ours writes it once, checks it with the VM, and spends its tokens on language. **Gates:** (1) ✓ the exposure curve (above); (2) tokens-to-recall — the trunk + store pair (the trunk routes, the store answers) ≥ 90% of held-out facts at one exposure where the trunk alone needs ≥ 100 (≥ 100× fewer fact tokens for the same recall); (3) the corpus accounting — the share of fact tokens in our cache (decode a sample, count entity / number / date tokens a store could hold), reported either way, ≥ 30% expected; (4) **the 40B-vs-1T test, scaled down** — two trunks of the same size (20M-class, a Colab job): one on the full text budget, one on 1/25 of it plus the store, the loop's textbooks and the input channels; the pair ≥ the full trunk on fact QA (through the loop), within 0.15 nats on the text held-out loss, ≥ 90% of it on BlockWorld tasks; (5) at size — the 450M continued with the fact link answers wiki-world questions at ≥ 2× the text control at the same tokens. **Kill:** gate 2 below 50% (the route does not learn; the host loop stays the only path), or gate 4's text loss worse by more than 0.4 nats (the textbooks do not close the law's gap; then the claim is facts only).
