[0012] The books as a source: passage index, frames, BookSource (attributed until corroborated), natural questions with tense and year

Built (CPU, no model, no network; the index and the books stay off-repo, tests use synthetic paragraphs):
- standin/books.py: SQLite FTS5 over the books' paragraphs (author / title from the file name, era / region /
  topic from the folders), a mention table of a world's names (a capitalised match; one-word names the books
  mostly write in lowercase are not mentions), incremental by file hash, twins read once, a cleaned mirror's
  copy before the original, historical_facts.jsonl as one book per era; passages(entity, words, era, k).
- standin/book_frames.py: facts read only where the sentence names the entity as the frame verb's subject
  (full name, or the last name with no capitalised word running into it; never a pronoun), with Wikidata's
  labels and the dates read beside them: birth / death date and place, burial, founded by, inception,
  location, part of, capital, point in time (a battle), reign and head of state (start-end).
- standin/book_source.py: BookSource (a learn.Source). One author -> attributed; two independent authors
  (credited people, none shared) -> plain; two values for a one-valued relation -> both held. citations
  (book, author, era, offset, sentence) kept for the record, times for the store.
- standin/question_forms.py (Nick's shape {wh} {tense} {verb form} {subject} [in {year}]?): forms per relation
  in three tenses, the hdc templates extended to three tenses, a parser back to (entity, relation, tense)
  (the most specific form wins; a tie between relations refuses), time_check (an ended fact asked in the
  present, a year outside its dates, a past event asked in the present: not answered), TemplateEmitter.
- cubbyllm/reasoning/learn.py: learn_and_answer(before=) asks sources ahead of `source` (the books before
  Wikidata); a source's `attributed` facts are stored "(attributed)"; a chain resting on one is refused
  attributed_only while the sources below are asked, a second, independent witness lifts it to plain, and
  with none the answer is returned verified with LearnResult.attributed (spoken hedged). Off (no before, no
  attributed): unchanged -- the existing loop tests pass as they were.
- standin/ask.py: AskLoop(books=, tense=): the books mounted ahead of the source, rec["tier"]="attributed"
  and rec["reply"]="One account says <answer>." (no source named), rec["evidence"] (internal, 0016's audit
  view), the question forms as a resolver and before the profile path, the time check (reason
  time_mismatch; on with the books, off without them unless tense=True). serve_api: --books, --tense.
- cubbyllm/reasoning/sleep.py: attributed_only -> latent_audit, time_mismatch -> clarify (the routing guard).
- standin/data/build_books_index.py, validation/exp_books_bench.py: the build and the acceptance bench.
