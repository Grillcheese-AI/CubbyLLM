[0035] B: why the copy did not fire on real villages: the gate stays shut on village documents, and their shape gives it no written token to copy

Measured without training: run 5's copy at every asking token (gate lambda, best match, what it points at), on 0034's
villages (cut, the placed questions, fixed) and beside the documents it was trained on.
Log: validation/logs/diag_s12_copy_2026-10-23.json, counts only.

1. The gate stays shut on village documents.
   - At 0034's `here` tokens: lambda 0.06 (things), 0.02 (names), 0.00 (nothing).
   - On run 5's own shard villages (spatial_here): 0.00 at all 359 asking tokens. The 0.953 there is the trunk's

2. The read mostly finds the right thing: at `here` it points at the answer's first piece, in its written form, for
   199 of 329 things.
3. 0034's document shape gives the copy nothing it can copy:
   - it asks at `here`, which is followed by "?" or a newline, not by the answer;
   - the answer's first token is a token never written: the listing writes "coal" at a line's start, and the answer
     after "Answer:" is " coal", another id;
   - with the lead token also asking, the gate opens (0.96 / 0.99) and still gives 0 of 329 things, 3 of 120 names.
4. In the far facts' shape (the lead token asks; "Answer:", a space, then the name in its written form), with no
   training:

   - the misses are mostly another piece of the same name (80 things, 69 names: a name's pieces all share one cell),
     few a neighbour's piece (10, 3);

So C as written (the copy's 1,027 parameters on village documents) can learn when to open, not which written token
the read points at. Its shard needs the far facts' shape: the lead token asking, the answers in the written form,
and each piece of a name at its own address. Questions in the order's report.
