[0037] Cue words: the question's capitalised words, a kind's own name, and none from words the world rarely uses

The real stack, with the rule on, lost "What river flows through Cairo?" (the Nile, with the rule off).
The cue words were "river", "flows" and "through". "flows" occurs in the world's values only in one album's
title, so it ruled out the city of Cairo (Q85), and the loop asked which.

- cue_words: the question's capitalised words after the first, less the name's and the relation's ("Nobel"
  in "What Nobel Prize did Marie Curie win?"). Not its verbs or prepositions. Not its head noun either: a
  head noun names the answer's class, which the subject's own values seldom use ("currency" is in no
  country's values).
- hdc_cues.CUE_MIN = 100: a word rules out only when that many of the world's subjects use it ("nobel": 711;
  "flows": 1). A word the world rarely uses rules out nothing, as a word it never uses already did.
- The cue table now also reads a kind's own name ("State" goes with U.S. states, "taxon" with taxa). The
  probe drew "taxon" from an item's instance of and ruled that same item out. The table only gains kinds, so
  a cue rules out less, never more.

replaces hdc_world.cues.sqlite once the two stack servers holding the old one are stopped.
