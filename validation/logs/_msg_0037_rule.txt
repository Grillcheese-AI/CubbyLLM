[0037] A shared name decided by the question's relation and its cue words (behind CB_NAMESAKE_RELATION=1, off)

The rule, built and tested. With the flag off, the hop is strict, as order 0036 left it.

- Kinds table (hdc_kinds): a kind carries a relation by its share. Its members' share must be at least
  LIFT=2 x the typed items' base rate, with NMIN=3 members.
  - The base comes from typed items only: untyped people dominate the snapshot, and an all-items base made
    humans carry nothing.
  - An item with no instance of is read from its specific relations: those at most SPREAD=50 kinds carry.
    Take the intersection of their kinds, else the union.
- Cue words (hdc_cues, new): a word-to-kinds table built once from the world's values. A cue word only
  rules out; a word the world never uses rules out nothing.
- The decision (hdc_relation, new; hdc_namesakes.hop_rows):
  - one item fits: the hop reads it;
  - several fit: they are noted, and none is read.
- The answer (ask_namesake_answer, new; ask_namesakes):
  - One fits: the answer, then at most two other namesakes named by their kind, with an offer.
  - A value the question's own description rules out is not said. "What Nobel Prize ...", with another
    award held, gives the new reason not_what_was_asked, routed to source_gaps in sleep.ROUTES.
    sleep.py is already over 300 lines; this is one line in its table.
  - Several fit, with a fact that tells them apart: each is walked pinned and labelled, in id order.
  - No such fact, or more than three: asked which, as before.
- ask.py: two hooks. seed_for gets the question and the plan's relations (for the cue words); after_walk gets
  the walk (to answer each).
