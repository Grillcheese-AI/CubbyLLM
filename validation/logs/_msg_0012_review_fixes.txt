[0012] The time rule, a same-name guard, finer places count (Cowork's review); 0006's run logs

Cowork's review reopened 0012 on one flat wrong answer: "the capital of the Kingdom of Kush is Kerma", on two
books -- true once, not for the kingdom's whole life.

- The time rule (standin/time_rule.py, new): a relation whose value changes over a subject's life (seeded from the
  Wikidata properties that take start / end qualifiers -- hdc's TIME_SENSITIVE -- plus capital, country, located
  in, part of and the wiki world's nouns) is said flat only when every book witness dates it; undated, it is hedged
  in time ("Kerma was a capital of Kush at one point."), and a year asked is refused (the tense check already
  refuses it, "the fact carries no dates"; with that check off the rule refuses it as time_undated, routed to
  clarify). It reads the books' citations and, for a book fact loaded from a store (order 0013's bulk worlds), the
  store's provenance and times. A world's own facts are dated by the world (the wiki world's present, hdc's "as
  of"): the rule does not read them.
- The same-name guard (book_source.fits): a book's person counts for the subject only if every birth or death year
  the book states for the name lies in the life the world knows (the loop's `_life`: the subject's birth and death
  years in its world; a life of at most 110 years). The book's era was tried as a test too and dropped: an era is
  a book's subject, not its people's -- on the bench it set aside Marie Tharp's, Mackintosh's, Agnesi's and Cayce's
  true birth years (ancient-history books cite modern scholars).
- Finer is agreement (validation/books_scoring.py, split out of exp_books_bench.py with agree / gazetteer /
  year): a place inside the gold scores as agreement; a coarser one is right only hedged; a capital lies in its
  state (the wiki world's CAPITAL_OF, hdc's capital). A value hedged "at one point" that is not the world's one
  value scores unconfirmed, not wrong. REVIEWED keeps a reviewer's verdict where the offline gazetteer cannot see
  the place (Gaiseric's Carthage: hdc's Vandal Kingdom has only Hippo Regius as capital), reported apart with the
  count as scored before review.
- exp_books_bench.py also takes --bulk <worlds dir> (order 0013: the bench with the bulk store loaded).
