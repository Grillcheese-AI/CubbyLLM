[0024] Events by day: news items grouped across outlets by their names and key words (no model)

standin/news_events.py (new, STANDALONE until the bench): one item through several feeds is one item (by
link); only kind news is grouped (the manifest's kind wins over a row's); two items are one event when
dated within 2 days and they share two names at Jaccard >= 0.15 over names and key words, or one name at
0.3; groups merge by average linkage (half the cross pairs linked); a name carried by more than 2% of the
items (Canada, Quebec, the UN) is not a name in common; a feed whose house style is title case gives no
names from its headlines (judged per feed: one name-dense headline is not a style). Each fix answers a
mistake seen on the first day's archive (a state visit chained to an election dispute by "Canada"; Nepal's
floods joined to Venezuela's earthquake by capitals). Constants were set by eye on that day, before any
judging: the 0.90 bar is for the judged sample of 100 groups after the week.
First day (2026-10-22 archive): 450 news items -> 418 groups, 30 of 2+ (62 items), 25 across outlets.
--sample N writes the judging file locally (it holds headlines). Tests: test_news_events.py (6).
