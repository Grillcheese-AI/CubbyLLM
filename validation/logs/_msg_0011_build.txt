[0011] Fact names in readable words (make_fact_shard --names v2) and facts_far1k_v2: event ids split with their case kept, no doubled word in any question

The wiki world's 77k event ids (FortPeckDam_ConstructionBegan1933) went into the shards as
"fortpeckdam constructionbegan1933": words() lowercased any name with an underscore, as it does a
relation. With --names v2 (validation/fact_names.py; --names v1, the default, renders every shard
before 0011 bit for bit: the first 300 documents of facts_far1k_lead rebuild identical):
- relations: as before (WORKS_FOR -> works for);
- entities: as before (KeikoMatsuzaka -> Keiko Matsuzaka), and an all-caps name is kept as it is
  (ABBA, 1 BCE, FIDO2: v1 lowercased these 1,979 names too);
- event ids: part by part, split on CamelCase and digit runs, case kept, joined by spaces:
  "Fort Peck Dam Construction Began 1933", "Rocket Use In China 13th Century". Nothing is lowercased,
  because an id cannot tell a word from a proper noun ("china" would be wrong; "Began" is only stiff).
79,810 of the 374,975 names render differently.

Questions: the 646 relations ending in "of" doubled the template's "of" ("What is the variant of of
Spanish Dancer?"). The order's suggested fix, dropping one "of", asks the inverse in English ("What is
the variant of Spanish Dancer?" asks for a variant OF it; "What is the capital of Paris?" for Paris
CAPITAL_OF France). So these ask subject first: "What is Spanish Dancer variant of?", with a "the"
where the name's last word is the relation's first ("What is Camp Album the album of?", 340 facts).
Over all 365,923 facts: questions with a doubled word outside the names, 101,246 (v1) -> 0 (v2).

facts_far1k_v2 (facts_far1k_lead's arguments, seed 2, --names v2; on the cache and the Drive):

27,585 -> 6 (titles beginning "Of" after the template's "of"); 0 with a doubled word outside a name.
