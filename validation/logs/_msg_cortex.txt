design: the advisor loop (G, P, H -> options, pros and cons, a pick or a question) (H-S11)

What the whole brain is for (Nick): given a goal G, past trials P and a
history of choices H, list the options that reach G with pros and cons,
pick the best by the user's criteria, or ask the one question whose
answer changes the pick. Mapped onto the design: options are branches
the forward model plays out, P is the postmortem memory, H reveals the
user's criteria, and a question is asked when the top branches swap on
an unweighted criterion. Learned in the sandbox, where every option and
claim is checked in a fork. H-S11: A1-A5 against the no-branching trunk
and a general language model.
