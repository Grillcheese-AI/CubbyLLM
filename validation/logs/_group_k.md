## 9d. Group K — Knowledge, the map, the state: the pause of 2026-10-20 (added 2026-10-20)

**Why this group exists.** Nick, 2026-10-20: "lets pause and do a thorough research … we need to find something that will really challenge everyone … plan / test some new hypothesis that are or seem to be way ahead … lets not be afraid of complex problems"; then: "the status is only for inspiration we wont take their research we will create our own"; "also search github for inspiration … some hidden gems are in small repos sometimes"; and two questions that turned into hypotheses of their own: "decoupling the information without trillion of tokens and still have the same power" (H-K7) and "kv cache is one of the problem when its massive its energy consuming a lot" (H-K8); and a repo to read, `google-research/swirl-dynamics` (H-K9). **The rule for this group:** the field's results are inspiration, named once per entry so the difference is explicit; every hypothesis is ours, stated in the algebra we already have (H-S12's Hilbert space: a unit vector per place, binding as motion, similarity as a kernel), with a pre-registered gate and a kill, and the cheap half of each run the same day where it could be. **The thread through all nine:** one space for place (H-S12), fact (H-K1), memory (H-K8) and time (H-K4), so what, where and when compose by binding; the trunk learns *when* to read (a gate, a query), the algebra guarantees *what* comes back, and the VM verifies before anything is spoken. A conventional model has none of these as objects — it has weights that may or may not have memorized them.

**What the field is doing, read for inspiration only (2026-10-20).** Test-time memory in the trunk: Titans (a neural memory updated by its own surprise), Titan-Llama (memory modules on a frozen Llama-3.2-1B, ~1% of the parameters trained), Nested Learning / HOPE (layers as learners at different update frequencies, a continuum memory), Gated DeltaNet-2 (erase and write decoupled, channel-wise, 1.3B/100B tokens), Kimi Linear and the Qwen hybrids (3:1 linear:attention), "Language Models Need Sleep" (offline passes that consolidate evicted context into fast weights), HeLa-Mem (Hebbian associative memory for agents); representation: "Attention as Binding" (attention is approximate VSA; explicit binding would be exact), Haga's disentangled successor information (grid cells and word embeddings from one factorization), grid-cell path integration as a group representation (`ruiqigao/grid-cell-path`), the capacity analysis of vector-symbolic superpositions (the d/(2 ln M) law); knowledge: "Physics of Language Models 3.3" (2 bits per parameter, ~1,000 exposures to store a fact), Memory Layers at Scale (product-key memory, 128B memory parameters), Hrrformer (holographic attention everywhere — linear time, lost accuracy); the bench the field now scores on: ARC-AGI-3 (interactive, hidden rules, Oct 2026 leaderboard: all closed models, the best in the sixties); and the small repos: `swirl-dynamics` (DySLIM — regularize a learned dynamics to keep the true system's invariant measure, so long rollouts stay on the attractor; flow-map matching and MeanFlow — one-step jumps over a time interval with the semigroup consistency Φ_{s→u} = Φ_{t→u} ∘ Φ_{s→t}; "Evolve Smoothly" — latent dynamics in the parameter space of a small network, kept low-dimensional by a consistency term; debiasing by optimal transport / rectified flow between a cheap model's distribution and the expensive one's; WENO-NN — a learned weight inside a numerical scheme that keeps the scheme's guarantees). **H-K1 — The fact store as a map: entities are places, relations are displacements, composition is path integration, relation discovery is unbinding — in the same Hilbert space the trunk binds places into.** Source: Nick, 2026-10-20; H-S12's encoding (`cubbyllm/spatial/ssp.py`). Inspiration: rotation-based knowledge-graph embeddings (RotatE) score a triple by a rotation per relation; ours uses the spatial link's phases (one space for the village and the graph), inverses are free (φ → −φ, so a backward hop is the same operator), composition is exact algebra (S(e0) ⊛ D1 ⊛ D2, never touching the store), and every proposal is verified by the VM before the loop speaks it — the map proposes, the walk checks. **What it brings:** "how is o related to s" by unbinding (D = S(o) ⊛ S(s)^-1, ranked over the 2,902 relations) — a question a conventional model answers from memorized text if at all; two hops in one read; the store's capacity in bytes, not tokens (H-K7). **Mechanism, built:** `validation/exp_k1_map.py` — entity phases (E, F) learned, relation phases (R, F) learned, score `mean_f cos(θ_s + φ_r − θ_o)`, softmax over the true answer and 8,191 shared negatives in both directions (SparseAdam on the 375k-row table: a touch is a step of `lr` radians), against TransE at the same width (2F real dims) and budget; the wiki world (`standin/data/out/wikikg`: 374,975 entities, 2,902 relations, 365,923 triples, 5% held out, 2,000 two-hop paths whose both hops are training triples). **Gates (pre-registered):** (1) composition — two-hop Hits@10 by path integration ≥ 0.6 × one-hop Hits@10; (2) relation discovery — unbinding Hits@3 ≥ 0.5 over 2,902 relations; (3) one hop — MRR within 10% of TransE's at the same width and budget. **Kill:** gate 3 worse than TransE by more than 30%, or gate 1 below 0.3. Status: **OPEN — running 2026-10-20 (F = 256, 12 epochs); first read below when it lands.**

**H-K2 — Surprise-gated episodic writing: the store keeps what the trunk did not predict, and erases what it later confirmed.** Source: Nick's "find something that will really challenge everyone" and H-E20's gate 2 (beyond-window recall at chance with a store that holds every aged token: 15.9% vs 16.8% shuffled). Inspiration: Titans (surprise drives the memory update), Gated DeltaNet-2 (erase and write as separate gates); ours is a *write mask on the row store we already have* — the token's own loss at training (its surprisal at serving) decides whether its (k, v) enters the store, the gate's later read error decides erasure, and the VM's verification is the confirmation signal on the host side. **What it brings:** a store of news — a needle is surprising by construction, filler is not; capacity scales with what was unexpected, not with length. **Advantage over conventional models:** a KV cache keeps everything and eviction heuristics score rows after the fact by attention mass; ours decides at write time by prediction error, which is the definition of information. **Mechanism (to build, flag-gated):** `CubbyMemory` with `CB_MEM_WRITE=surprise` — rows ranked by the token's loss within each window, the top `mem_write_k` kept, the rest never written; erase on a confirmed read (the read's output matched the next token's logit). **Gates:** (1) the needle probe (`exp_needle_forward.py`, 4,096, three depths): beyond-window recall ≥ 50% with a store of 256 rows chosen by surprise where the same 256 rows chosen by recency sit at chance (< 20%); (2) held-out text loss within 0.01 of the ungated store; (3) the store's row count at 4,096 ≤ 1/8 of the ungated one. **Kill:** surprise-chosen rows no better than recency-chosen by 10 points. Status: **OPEN — not built; runs on the grown model's second round.**

**H-K3 — Continual learning by construction: a frozen trunk, and a sleep pass that distills the day's VM-verified facts into the context adapter's bases, per context.** Source: Nick, 2026-10-20; H0 (the context channel), H-A7 (the learning gate). Inspiration: Nested Learning (levels at different update frequencies), "Language Models Need Sleep" (offline consolidation of evicted context); ours consolidates *only what the VM verified*, into the adapter the architecture already has, with the learning gate (H-A7) deciding whether the update is CE-neutral before it is kept, and the trunk's goldens unchanged by construction (the trunk is frozen; features off = bit-identical). **What it brings:** a model that is better tomorrow at what it checked today, for an adapter-sized update, with forgetting measured as a number rather than hoped away. **Advantage:** conventional models retrain, fine-tune with drift, or retrieve; ours learns facts by writing rows and consolidates them by sleeping, with the trunk's language untouched. **Gates:** (1) after one sleep pass over 1,000 verified facts, recall of those facts through the adapter alone (store removed) ≥ 80%; (2) text held-out loss within 0.01; (3) the previous day's 1,000 facts recalled ≥ 70% after the next day's pass (forgetting bounded). **Kill:** gate 1 below 50% or gate 3 below 40%. Status: **OPEN — not built; the sleep pass is `exp_if_follow.py`'s sibling (H-E20 gate 4).**

**H-K4 — A clock in the state: a one-dimensional fractional-power time code bound into memory keys and the scene, so before / after / when are displacement queries.** Source: Nick, 2026-10-20; H-S12 (the same encoding, dims = 1); Group P (keyframes need a coordinate). Inspiration: RoPE is this code inside attention only; ours is a readable coordinate the trunk, the host loop and the VM share, and a what–where–when triple is one bound vector `e ⊛ S(p) ⊛ T(t)`. **What it brings:** temporal questions by algebra — "what happened between t1 and t2" is a box kernel read (H-S12's closed form), "which came first" is a sign. **Gates:** (1) a synthetic diary (events with times): between-t1-and-t2 recall ≥ 90% through the box read; (2) in the trunk, with `T(t)` bound into the episodic keys, the needle probe's beyond-window recall at 4,096 at least the untimed store's, and "which came first" questions ≥ 80% where the untimed store sits at chance. **Kill:** gate 2's needle recall drops by more than 5 points (the clock costs the read). Status: **OPEN — the host side is a `Scene` with dims = 1 (cheap, next); the trunk side follows H-K2.**

**H-K5 — Interactive hidden-rule learning in BlockWorld: a private ARC-AGI-3-like bench where the rule is a chemistry the agent must act to discover, propose as a program, and verify with the VM.** Source: Nick ("something that will really challenge everyone"); ARC-AGI-3 (interactive, hidden rules; the field's current yardstick). Inspiration: the bench's shape; ours is in our own world (BlockWorld's `VoxelGrid`, chemistry, `orders.py`), scored by the VM, never by a model. **What it brings:** a measurable test of learning-by-doing — propose → act → verify — where verification is in the loop, not in the weights, and the transcript of actions is the evidence. **Advantage:** a frontier model given the same observations must guess the rule from text; the loop runs the experiment. **Gates:** (1) 20 hidden rules; the loop recovers ≥ 60% with ≤ 50 actions each; (2) a frontier model (when OpenRouter credit exists), given the same observation transcripts and the same action budget, scores ≤ the loop + 10 points; (3) every rule the loop states is VM-verified before it is spoken (0 wrong spoken). **Kill:** the loop below 30%. Status: **OPEN — not built; the rule generator is the first piece (`cubby-blockworld`, private).**

**H-K6 — Provable binding heads: an explicit bind / unbind head with unitary role vectors in place of some attention heads, so role-filler structure is a theorem where attention has a tendency.** Source: "Attention as Binding" (attention ≈ VSA, approximately); H-B6 (the trunk is already shaped to be bindable). Ours is the exact version: roles are unitary (`SpatialLink.unitary`), binding is the circular convolution the spatial link uses, unbinding is its conjugate, and a role-swap is a different vector by construction. **What it brings:** who-did-what-to-whom that cannot be confused by swapping the names; composition that survives nesting. **Gates:** (1) on a role-filler task with swapped roles, a bind head ≥ 95% where an attention head of the same width ≤ 80% at the same training budget; (2) text loss within 0.02 with 1 of 4 heads replaced. **Kill:** gate 2 fails. Status: **OPEN — not built.**

**H-K7 — The knowledge budget split: a trunk's tokens should buy language and reasoning; facts live in the store, written once.** Source: Nick, 2026-10-20 ("decoupling the information without trillion of tokens and still have the same power"). Inspiration: the exposure law ("Physics of Language Models 3.3": ~2 bits per parameter, a fact needs ~1,000 exposures to be stored), RETRO and kNN-LM (retrieval as knowledge), Memory Layers at Scale; ours: the store is H-K1's map plus the exact store, algebraic and verified, not a dense index, and the trunk's part is a *route* (a gate and a query, as in H-S12), not the facts. **The arithmetic, stated:** the wiki world's 374,975 entities cost 2 KB each in the map (F = 256 phases) — 0.8 GB and one write per fact; in weights, ~1,000 exposures × ~20 tokens ≈ 7.5B training tokens for the same facts, and they cannot be changed without training again. **What it brings:** a model you teach by writing a row; the tokens go to language. **Advantage:** orders of magnitude fewer tokens for the same facts, and facts that update without a gradient step. **Gates:** (1) the exposure curve on our own trunk — tiny trunks trained on fact sentences at k ∈ {1, 10, 100, 1,000} exposures per fact: exact recall < 5% at k = 1 and ≥ 80% at k = 1,000 (the law holds here too); (2) the trunk + store pair (the trunk routes, the store answers) ≥ 90% of held-out facts at one exposure — the write; (3) at size — the 450M continued with the fact link (H-K1's phases bound into entity tokens at the input, read mid-trunk like the spatial link) answers wiki-world questions at ≥ 2× the text control at the same tokens. **Kill:** gate 2 below 50% (the route does not learn; the host loop stays the only path). Status: **OPEN — gate 1 is a CPU afternoon (`exp_k7_exposure.py`, to write); gate 3 waits on H-S12's gate 1 (same mechanism, places → entities).**

**H-K8 — A holographic state in place of the KV cache: beyond the window, one vector per chunk of C tokens, the superposition of the chunk's bound (key, value) pairs with unitary keys, read by unbinding; bytes per token flat in context length.** Source: Nick, 2026-10-20 ("kv cache is one of the problem when its massive its energy consuming a lot … if we are able to do something like that it would be immense power in the end"). Inspiration: Hrrformer (holographic attention everywhere; linear time, lost accuracy on language), linear-attention states (Gated DeltaNet-2, Kimi Linear: a d×d matrix per layer), the VSA capacity analyses; ours keeps exact attention inside the window (512), goes holographic only beyond it, in chunks with a summary key for selection, in the same algebra as place and fact (`SpatialLink.unitary`, `bind`, `unbind` are the operators), and — from `swirl-dynamics`' DySLIM — asks that the state's statistics stay stationary over long rollouts. **The law, derived and measured (`validation/exp_k8_holo.py`, 2026-10-20):** with random unitary keys and unit values every decoy's dot with a read is N(0, C/d), its maximum over N candidates ~ √(2 C ln N / d), so a nearest-value decode over N holds while C ln N < d/2 (one vector alone: M ln M < d/2). Measured: the law's crossing is where the decode sits at 0.68–0.70 at every d (512 / 1024 / 2048) — gate 1 as written (90% within 25% of the law) **fails**, and the corrected rule is that 90% holds to about half the law (M = 31 / 55 / 97). The chunk store at N = 4,096: at d = 1024 (the grown model's width) chunks of 16 decode 1.00 / 1.00 / 0.97 over query noise σ = 0 / 0.5 / 1.0, chunks of 32 0.97 / 0.90 / 0.53 (**gate 2, C = 32, σ = 0.5: 0.90, at the line**), chunks of 64 0.61 / 0.51 / 0.14; at d = 2048 chunks of 32 decode 1.00 / 1.00 / 0.98 and 64 0.97 / 0.95 / 0.64; summing all chunks (no selection) decodes nothing (0.00) and top-4 summed is worse than top-1 — selection is the whole mechanism; the read is attenuated (cos 0.16–0.25 to the true value) though decodable. **Bytes per generated token at the grown geometry (D = 1024, L = 64, window 512, 16 memory layers), bf16:** full attention at N = 1M touches 262 GB per token; the window + row store we have, 2.2 GB (resident 35 GB); window + chunks (C = 32), 1.15 GB (resident 2.2 GB); at N = 4,096 all three sit on the 64-layer window's floor of 128 MB — the energy argument is a long-context argument, and the resident saving (16×) is the larger one. **What it brings:** a context of a million tokens held in 2 GB and read at a cost flat in its length. **Advantage:** a KV cache's per-token bytes grow with N and so does its energy; ours is bounded by construction, and the beyond-window read is one Fourier-domain product, no softmax over rows. **Gates:** (1) **failed as written** (the law marks the 0.68 point; recorded, not re-registered); (2) **passed at the line** (0.90); (3) model level, to build — a tiny trunk with the holographic store (`mem_kind=holo`, flag-gated) on a synthetic recall task matches the row store within 5 points and beats window-only by 30; (4) the grown model's variant at 4,096: needle recall ≥ the row store's with ≤ 1/16 of its resident bytes; (5) stationarity — the state's norm and the read's SNR drift < 10% over 100k tokens of rollout (DySLIM's question, asked of our state). **Kill:** gate 3 fails (the trunk cannot use a 0.16-cos read; then rows stay, chunks go). Status: **OPEN — gates 1–2 run (`validation/logs/exp_k8_holo_2026-10-20.json`); gate 3 is the next build (a `CubbyMemory` sibling, < 300 lines, off by default).**

**H-K9 — Jumps in time with a composition law: a learned flow map Φ_k on the trunk's state that gives the state k steps ahead in one application, trained with the semigroup consistency Φ_j ∘ Φ_k = Φ_{j+k}, so prospection's keyframes cost a read, not a rollout.** Source: `google-research/swirl-dynamics` (flow-map matching, MeanFlow: one-step jumps over a time interval with the consistency of the flow; DySLIM; "Evolve Smoothly"), read at Nick's request 2026-10-20; Group P (keyframes, branching futures); H-S12 (composition is binding — the displacement algebra already composes exactly, the learned part is the trunk's dynamics). **What it brings:** a 64-token lookahead for one application instead of 64 forwards; a planner that can afford to look; the energy argument applied to thinking ahead. **Advantage:** conventional models plan by generating; ours would jump in state space and verify the landing with the VM. **Gates:** (1) on BlockWorld traces, Φ_k's state after k = 8 / 32 actions decodes the right world facts ≥ 80% (the rollout's own decode = 100%); (2) consistency error ‖Φ_j(Φ_k(s)) − Φ_{j+k}(s)‖ / ‖s‖ < 0.1 on held-out traces; (3) planner success with keyframes ≥ 90% of token-by-token rollout at ≤ 1/8 the forwards. **Kill:** gate 1 below 50%. Status: **OPEN — not built; traces come from `cubby-blockworld` (private).**
