[0014] in progress: the hdc set's Wikidata5M facts as a disk-backed world behind the wiki world (the code; the labels are being fetched)

- standin/data/fetch_hdc_labels.py: the 1,599,821 Q-ids the local table cannot name, asked of the Wikidata
  API 50 at a time, in series (maxlag, a User-Agent), labels en+fr, appended as they arrive (resumable; Nick
  chose the API over a dump, 2026-10-22). Running: ~32,000 requests, ~0.74 s each.
- standin/data/hdc_world.py: every triplet column of every file (3,295,347 Q-id statements, 999,441 alias
  statements) and the multi-hop rows' `facts` sentences, labelled (the local table, the API's answers, the
  properties' JSON; edited labels back to the property's), deduplicated, into SQLite with a names table
  (labels, aliases, Q-ids); dropped facts counted by reason; TIME_SENSITIVE properties (heads of state and
  government, officeholders, populations, teams, employers, CEOs, ...); the wiki world's facts on the same
  subject and relation counted as agreeing or contradicting.
- standin/hdc_store.py: HdcWorld (read-only; a subject by label, alias or Q-id; hop as TripleIndex.hop answers;
  an LRU, no facts in RAM) and Stores (the wiki world first, then the hdc world: merged hop, _by_subj,
  _by_obj, paths, relations; writes, provenance and dates are the wiki world's; a hdc fact's provenance is its
  snapshot's).
- standin/ask.py: the loop's relations include the other worlds' (by count, not by parsing 4.3M facts); a
  changing value from a snapshot is said "As of 2019, X." (tier as_of), flat when 2019 is asked, refused for
  another year or the future (time_mismatch); profile_ask reads every ' of ' prefix as a possible relation
  ("the head of state of X" is a relation ask, not a profile -- also "place of birth", "date of death").
- standin/serve_api.py: --world hdc (CUBBY_HDC_WORLD).
