Cubby450mTalk from cubbyllm.emit and LlamaCppEmitter from standin.emitter_llama (the --gguf seat, one

fast-word encoder stays loaded by file path, lazily, as before (question 8 is open).

interpreter loads no standin/, cubby_serve/ or validation/ module, and neither torch nor grilly.
