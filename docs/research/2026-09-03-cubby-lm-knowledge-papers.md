# cubby-lm/external/knowledge — ten papers mapped to our mechanisms (2026-09-03)

The owner's paper folder in the predecessor repo (`C:\Users\grill\Documents\GitHub\cubby-lm\external\knowledge`,
ten PDFs, arXiv IDs from the first pages). Each row says what the paper is, which hypothesis or mechanism here it
touches, and what — if anything — it changes. Concepts only; nothing here is validated by citation
(`CLAUDE.md`: a source document asserting something confidently is not VERIFIED).

| paper | what it is | touches | what it changes here |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Code2LoRA** — Hypernetwork-generated adapters for code LMs under software evolution (Hotsko, Li, Deng, Nie; arXiv 2606.06492, Jun 2026) | a hypernetwork turns a repository snapshot into a LoRA adapter (zero inference-time token overhead); **Code2LoRA-Evo** keeps a GRU hidden state updated per commit diff, so the recurrence *augments* the snapshot adapter as the codebase evolves. 63.8% cross-repo exact match, matching the per-repository LoRA upper bound; Evo +5.2 pp over a shared LoRA | **H0 (θ = f(c))**, the adapter lifecycle (`standin/README.md` § v8) | The closest published shape to our lifecycle: the static adapter is our *spawn* from a partition; the Evo state updated per diff is the *continuous* form of "created when needed" — an adapter that tracks a changing context instead of being re-spawned. Two things to take: (1) the conditioning input is a *repository* (long, structured), not a task description — the same move as our world-mixture-as-context; (2) their benchmark has a **temporal holdout** (92 repos created after the scrape cutoff) — the right eval shape for our history/era families and for any adapter that claims to track drift. |
| **Less is MoE / Fisher-MoE** — trimming experts in domain-specialist LMs (He, Zou, Jiang, Ding, Qu, Li, Miller; CMU/UCSD/MIT; arXiv 2606.05538, Jun 2026) | expert-level MoE compression collapses on GSM8K/HumanEval/MATH; capability is concentrated in a **tiny set of FFN intermediate dimensions** (12 of 1.35M in Qwen1.5-MoE) found by Fisher importance; pruning at that granularity keeps capability at 50% compression, −45% weight memory, +21% throughput | the lifecycle's **prune** step; the cycle-two tag-routed MoE in the 2B runbook; H-A7's harness list | Prune by Fisher importance at the dimension level, not by expert/adapter usage counts alone — our `idle_roles` (calls under a line) is the coarse version; a spawned adapter that is never called is prunable, but a *used* adapter's dead dimensions are where the memory is. Also a warning we already learned the hard way: commonsense-style evals hide the damage; the VM eval on arithmetic/programs is exactly the sensitive kind. |
| **LoopMoE** — iterative computation + MoE (Chen, Li, Huang, Yin, Shang, Qin; HKUST-GZ/Huawei; arXiv 2606.04438, Jun 2026) | a looped sparse-MoE: a shared block iterated with **Iter-AdaLN** (modulation conditioned on iteration index + hidden state) and a capacity-balancing rule for the attention-to-FFN active-parameter ratio; beats a matched vanilla MoE on 8/9 benchmarks at 3B, holds at 9B | the hybrid backbone's recurrence (H-D4/H-P3), the cycle-two MoE, H-P4 (recompute from keyframe) | A controlled result that depth-by-iteration and capacity-by-sparsity are separable axes. For us it is a *cycle-two* input: if the tag-routed MoE upcycle happens, looping the shared block is the cheap way to buy depth without parameters. Note the modulation signal is (iteration index, hidden state) — self-conditioned, the pattern our H0 context channel must not copy (`standin/README.md`, the MindForge wiring gap). |
| **OOWM** — Object-Oriented Programmatic World Modeling for embodied reasoning (Chen, Lin, Wang; Sun Yat-sen; arXiv 2604.09580, Feb 2026) | replaces free-text CoT with a **State Abstraction (UML class diagram) + Control Policy (UML activity diagram)** — a symbolic, executable world model; trained SFT → **GRPO with outcome-based rewards on the final plan**; MRoom-30k | the GoT plan (`standin/plan_suggestions.md`), the CoT-in-CubeLang spec, the GRPO rung (`TODO.md` v9), cubby-man's world | Independent confirmation of the design we chose: reasoning as a typed, executable structure rather than prose, and SFT-then-GRPO with outcome rewards. Our version is stronger where it matters — the structure is a CubeLang program the VM *executes and verifies* (their UML is checked for coherence, not run) — and weaker where they are: they condition on a visual observation; our world is facts. Their three-stage pipeline (SFT on structure, then GRPO on outcome) is the v8e → v9 sequence. |
| **SVAF** — Symbolic-Vector Attention Fusion for collective intelligence (Xu, SYM.BOT; arXiv 2604.03955, Apr 2026) | agents in different domains exchange signals decomposed into **7 typed semantic fields**, each evaluated by a learned fusion gate (redundant / aligned / guarded / rejected) and remixed; the gate discovers **mood as the highest-weight field** by epoch 1; per-agent closed-form continuous-time network whose learned per-neuron time constants give fast/slow dynamics; 78.7% three-class on 237K samples; 7-node live mesh | MoWM inter-world delegation, the affect layer (§5.5), H-P3 (time constants), the contradiction ideas from the GoT round | The typed-field gate is a concrete shape for **inter-world disagreement**: not "which world wins" but "which fields of the other world's answer are absorbed, guarded or rejected" — a finer version of our tie-breaker-or-abstain rule, and one where the VM could certify per field. The mood-first finding is the paper-side version of our own: the hormonal state is part of c. The per-neuron time constants echo H-P3's τ spectrum (ours collapsed to 0.5–1.8 steps in the recurrence; theirs are learned to span seconds to indefinite). |
| **Hyperdimensional Decoding of SNNs** (Kinavuidi, Peres, Rhodes; Manchester; arXiv 2511.08558, Nov 2025; Neuromorphic Computing and Engineering) | an SNN that **outputs hypervectors decoded by HDC**; better accuracy, lower latency, 1.2–3.7× less energy than rate/latency decoding on DvsGesture / SL-Animals-DVS; **identifies 100% of samples from an unseen class** | the vision cortex's SNN → VSA bridge (`cubemind/perception/snn.py`), the fast route's SNN slot, the lifecycle's **novelty** source | Exactly the bridge cubemind built (spikes → block-code projection) with a published decoding recipe and one result we need: HDC decoding **detects unknown classes** — that is the input-side "a context no specialist has seen" signal, measured rather than assumed. Port target for the SNN slot's readout. |
| **Dual Triangle Attention** — bidirectional attention without positional embeddings (Hallee, Gleghorn; Delaware/Synthyra; arXiv 2604.18603, Apr 2026) | split each head's query-key subspace into two triangular masks (past+self, future+self) so a bidirectional transformer keeps the causal mask's implicit positional signal; one `flex_attention` kernel, no extra parameters; best context extension with RoPE | H-D4's windowed attention (FlexAttention is already our sliding-window path, H-E4) | Not for the decoder trunk (causal already carries position). Relevant if a bidirectional encoder is ever built for the retrieval head or a routing encoder (M2/M3's "the trunk is not a retrieval encoder"): it would come without positional embeddings and with the same kernel we use. |
| **Near-Free Parallelism** — how much parallel decoding is free (He, Zhang, Liu, Zhou, Liu; WeChat AI/PKU; arXiv 2605.30851, May 2026) | isolates the *system-side* capacity for multi-position decoding (speculative, MTP, diffusion): the NFP boundary is set by hardware balance and kernel granularity, and the idle-compute intuition over-predicts it by up to 23× | serve latency; the frontier walk's cost model; H-E4 | A budget rule for anything that decodes several candidates per step (the frontier walk, GRPO's K samples, fork handles): measure the NFP boundary on the serving card before assuming extra positions are free. On the RX 6750 XT through llama.cpp that number is unknown; it belongs in the same microbenchmark as Grilly2's four ops. |
| **VQ-SAD** — Vector-quantized structure-aware diffusion for molecules (Noravesh, Haffari, Soon, Pal; arXiv 2605.00354, May 2026) | atom and bond types as **discrete codes of a VQ-VAE codebook** used as tokenizers for a graph diffusion model; the learned discrete vocabulary avoids fingerprint hash collisions and balances rare types | H-C1/H-C2 (dynamic vocabulary), the block-code codebook (agenda Q3: warm-start codebooks from our table, fine-tune through the quantizer) | The same move as Q3 — a learned discrete codebook as the vocabulary of a structured domain — from a molecule paper. Reinforces the agenda's plan; adds nothing to the LM side directly. |
| **Bratrix** — deep semantic uncertainty perception for language-anchored vision–brain alignment (Feng et al.; SJTU/NTU/ZJU; arXiv 2511.04078, Nov 2025) | aligns EEG/MEG/fMRI with visual and linguistic embeddings in a shared latent space, with an **uncertainty-aware weighting** of noisy neural signals; +14.3% on 200-way EEG retrieval | the perception cortex, the affect layer's "signals, not feelings" discipline | Far from the trunk. The transferable idea is the uncertainty-weighted alignment of a noisy modality — the shape a perception cortex would use to weight its own readouts before they reach the hormones. Filed, not queued. |

## What to carry forward

- **Code2LoRA-Evo is the paper to cite when the lifecycle's continuous form is specced**: an adapter that
  *tracks* a context through a recurrent state updated per change, rather than re-spawning — and its
  temporal-holdout benchmark is the eval shape for anything that claims to follow drift.
- **OOWM is the outside confirmation of SFT → GRPO on structured, outcome-rewarded reasoning**; ours differs in
  that the structure executes on a verifier.
- **Fisher-MoE** moves the prune step from "unused adapters" to "unused dimensions", and confirms that the
  sensitive evals (arithmetic, code) are the ones to trust.
- **HDC-SNN decoding** gives the vision cortex's SNN → VSA bridge a published readout with unknown-class
  detection — the novelty source the lifecycle's detector needs.
- **SVAF's typed-field gate** is a candidate for per-field inter-world arbitration when the two-world harvest
  exists.
- **Near-Free Parallelism** says: measure the multi-candidate decoding boundary on the serving card before the
  frontier walk or GRPO's K samples are budgeted as free.
