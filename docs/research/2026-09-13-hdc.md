# 2026-09-13 — hdc: a rephrased multi-hop set with the facts on record, through the gate

Nick's list of datasets to absorb (2026-09-12) included `E:\datasets\hdc` — nace-ai's hypernet-scaling-law
data over Wiki5M: 1/2/3-hop question–answer pairs with their facts, a relation → question-template map
(278 relations), and `ood_validation_rephrased.pq`: **10,000 questions, each in its canonical template
wording and in an LLM's rephrasing**, with the chain's facts. The canonical wording is the grammar's own
basin ("What is the continent of the country of the country of citizenship of X?"); the rephrasing is free
text ("Which continent does the country of citizenship of X belong to?"). That is a matched-pair set at
10,000 with the facts on record — the store can hold every chain, so a refusal is the coverage rule's,
never the store's — and, certified, a source of gen-3 records with two- and three-hop free-text wordings
the deterministic builder has few of.

## The probe (`validation/exp_r18_hdc_rephrased.py`)

The facts come in the templates' noun form (`A is the country B is in`); they are parsed with the dataset's
own mapping, the most specific relation winning, and a fact no template parses or a chain whose hops do not
join is skipped, never guessed: **8,840 of 10,000 chains** parse (358 an unparsed fact, 679 broken, 123 whose
answer is not the chain's object). The store holds all their facts under Wikidata's labels (11,774 facts,
195 relations). Two planners, one gate. **Host**: the plan is the seed and the question's own words for each
hop's label — the longest n-gram the property table resolves to that property, longest label first, never a
gram ending in `of` unless it is the label (Wikidata lists the inverse reading as an alias: `father of` under
*child*), never one of hdc's own edited words the table has no form of (`component` for *has part*: tried,
it only moved the refusal to `unknown_relation`). **Emitter**: the gen-2 emitter's plan, the generality gate
on matched pairs (exp_r9's design at 10,000). Kill line 0 wrong, both.

## What the first 60 rows found — three gate defects, each pinned

1. **A relation that ends in `of`.** `instance of` — Wikidata's commonest label, 3,611 of the 10k's facts —
   put in a tail gives `instance of of X`, whose two ` of `s overlap at one space; `str.split(' of ')` sees
   the first and read `instance` | `of X`: an unknown relation nobody could resolve, and 121 fragment
   relations in the store's vocabulary. `planner.of_prefixes` offers every prefix, overlapping ones
   included, and every tail split — the held tier, the paraphrase tier, the question-decided tier, the fact
   parse, the hop-0 readings — now uses it; the store's vocabulary fell from 316 relations to 195.
2. **The overlap tier read the inverse relation.** `administrative territorial entity` is P131's own alias
   (`located in the administrative territorial entity`); the walk's paraphrase tier (Jaccard ≥ 0.6) matched
   it first to P150, `contains administrative territorial entity` — **the inverse** — and a store holding
   both directions would have verified the child for a question asking the parent. Lever 4 now runs
   **ahead of the first walk** for any plan wording the store does not hold exactly: the table's one held
   label is taken before any overlap can be, and a wording the table says names two held labels is refused
   as the ambiguity it always was, never the overlap's pick. Exact evidence outranks overlap.
3. **The split wording** (`covers()` v6). On the rephrased questions the largest refusal class was a relation
   the question words with all of its content words, not contiguously: `Which country does X hold
   citizenship in?` for *country of citizenship*, `What languages are spoken, written, or signed by X?` for
   *languages spoken, written or signed* — 3,463 of the 8,840. A relation the contiguous tiers cannot place
   is now claimed word by word, each word outside every other claim, in any order; the residual rule runs on
   what is left exactly as before, so a dropped hop still fails; a relation worded contiguously but out of
   order is a swapped chain and is still refused; and the documented limit is that a split wording carries
   no order, so the swapped chain of a *split* question passes coverage and it is the walk that refuses it.
   With it, a where-question's verbs of location (`found`, `located`, `situated`) are frame words like its
   class noun: `In which country can X be found?` had died on `found`, a word of `found in taxon`.

What stays refused is the dataset's and the table's, not the host's: two- and three-hop coverage refusals
whose words are all there (`plan_does_not_cover_question`, 720 and 474 rephrased — a hop's wording that the
table lists as a split alias the gate's lever 6 cannot yet see, `has component` for *has part*; the possessive

(an inversion of *parent taxon*), `In which language was the film originally produced?`, and multi-valued
hops (`ambiguous_hop`, 183 and 225), which are a list-answer shape, not a coverage one.

A fourth rule came out of the battery, not the probe. **The place ask had over-reached**: with the class
noun `location` taken out of the vocabulary for every place question, exp_r9's 'What location does the
location of X have?' let a one-hop plan cover a two-hop question and the walk spoke the inner hop — one
wrong answer, and a second on 'What is the location of the instance of X?'. Now a class noun followed by
`of` is the relation, not an ask (`what is the location of` names *location*), and the asked span is
stripped from the text only when its noun occurs once, so a second `location` is a hop the plan must
claim. Pinned; exp_r9 back to 228 / 0.

## The emitter arm (gen 2, 600 chains × 2 wordings) — two more rules, and a temporal residue

The first run spoke **11 wrong** of 1,200 — the kill line, and the most useful output of the day. Read:

* **Five were the rephraser's, not the gate's**: 'Which genre does The Orange Humble Band belong to?' for
  'What is the instance of the genre of X?' — the rephrasing dropped the chain's last hop, the emitter
  answered the question as written (Alt-Rock), and the dataset's gold is the longer chain's (styles of
  music). Such rows are now detected (the last hop has no wording in the rephrasing) and counted, not
  scored: 265 of the 8,840.
* **Two hid a dropped hop behind an inflection**: 'How is the flag of the country of X depicted?' planned
  as [country, flag] — `depicted` is no relation word, `depicts` is; 'the history of the topic of the
  membership of the country' planned without `member of`. The residual rule now has a **stem tier**: a
  held relation ALL of whose content words are left over by their stems (`depict`, `memb`) is a dropped
  hop — a whole relation, not one word of a longer one, so 'associated' does not read as `genetic
  association` (that over-reach was tried and measured: 700 false refusals on the host arm).
* **One was the place ask again**: 'In which country is the location of formation of X?' asks for the
  country OF that location; with 'country' stripped as the ask, a one-hop plan spoke Minneapolis. The
  asked class noun is now stripped only when the store does not hold it as a relation, exactly; 'country'
  and 'location' are relations of this store, so they are hops the plan must claim — and 'In which
  country was X born?' is rightly refused for a plan that stops at the place of birth (a city), where
  'In which city was X born?' is not (`city` is a word of `capital city`, not a relation). The reverse
  mixed reading covers the two-hop plan for that shape (outer hop as the ask before the entity, inner
  hop as the verb).
* **One remains, and is temporal**: 'Which individual occupies the position of the head of government in
  the country of Active (1850)?' — the gold path is *office held by head of government → officeholder*
  (Turnbull); the emitter's *country → head of government* found Gorton, a head of government of
  Australia at another time. Both chains are true; the question binds no time; the store's one value per
  chain kept the ambiguity rule silent. The honest answer is a qualifier the plan cannot yet carry
  (the 'as of' class from exp_r11), on record as the residue.

Second run, the rules in (`_gen2`, final): **0 wrong of 1,180 scored** (20 dropped-hop rephrasings set aside);
rephrased 127 / 318 one-hop, 43 / 172 two-hop, 5 / 90 three-hop; original 210 / 318, 68 / 187, 9 / 95;
matched pairs both 171, original only 116, rephrased only 4, neither 309. The temporal case is refused
too now — `position` is a word of *position held*, a relation the store holds, and the plan had not
claimed it. What the gen-2 emitter mostly dies of here is `unknown_relation` (127 / 93 on the one-hops):
it names hdc's relations in words the table has no form of (`component`, `instance`) or in the grammar's
own joints, and the host does not invent. The gen-3 emitter, trained on `gen3_hdc`, is the one to run
this arm with.
