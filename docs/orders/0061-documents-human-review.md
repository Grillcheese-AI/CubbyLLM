# 0061 — Documents with human review: fill only what can be read and checked, and send the rest to a person
status: open (queue: after 0060)
written: 2026-11-07 by Cowork · approved: 2026-11-07 by Nick ("yes", after his invoice example) · hypothesis: the regulated-work claim (0058), with 0049's refusal routing

## Why
Nick's example is an invoice where one line item is blurred. The total reads $500.00 and the visible line reads
$450.00. A model filled the gap with $50.00 to make it balance, the check refused it, and a financial auditor read the
value from the scan and entered it, with their id on record. Reading invoices and forms is regulated, high-stakes work
where one invented number costs money, so "refuse, route to a person, record who fixed it" is the right behaviour.

**What it brings, in plain words:** Cubby reads invoices and forms and fills in only what it can actually read and
check. Anything unreadable goes to a person, with the exact spot on the page and a suggested value clearly marked as a
calculation, not a reading.
- **Over a conventional model:** an AI extractor fills a blurred field with a plausible number, and the books are
  wrong without anyone knowing.

## Build
0. **Licences first.** The evaluation sets are CORD (receipts), SROIE (receipts) and FUNSD (forms), each licence
   checked and recorded. Non-commercial sets go to research runs only, and no model-written label is used.
1. **Reading.** An OCR tool turns each page into regions (bounding boxes) with their text and per-character
   confidence. The tool and its version are recorded with every reading, and its licence is checked.
   - The repo's encyclopedia reader (`standin/encyclopedia.py`) is the precedent: scanned pages become facts tagged
     with volume, headword and sentence, and each OCR hazard is a rule.
2. **Field proposals.** A proposer maps regions to the fields of a document schema. For an invoice: supplier, number,
   date, purchase order, lines, subtotal, tax, total.
   - **The guard:** every number and name in a proposed field must appear in the text read from its region.
   - A field with no readable value is refused as "unreadable" and is never filled.
3. **Exact checks:**
   - amounts and dates in the right format;
   - the lines add up to the subtotal, and the subtotal plus tax to the total, to the cent;
   - the purchase order exists, and its amounts agree;
   - the supplier exists, by id, through 0058's GLEIF link where it applies;
   - the invoice number has not been entered before;
   - the dates make sense (the due date is not before the invoice date).
4. **Derived suggestions.** When arithmetic pins a missing value (the total minus the known lines), the reviewer sees
   it labelled "derived, not read", with its working. It is never committed on its own, since the total may be wrong
   or a line may be missing.
5. **Human review.** A review task shows the refused fields, the image region of each, and any derived suggestion.
   - **The reviewer's entry is a source:** it is stored with their role, their reviewer id, the time and the region
     they looked at.
   - **It still goes through the exact checks.** If the entry fails them (the invoice still doesn't add up), the task
     stays open with the reason.
   - Above an amount set by Nick, a second approval is required.
6. **Commit.** A document's values are committed only when every field is either read and checked, or reviewed. Each
   value keeps its provenance: the region and the reader's version, or the reviewer.
7. **The loop (0049).**
   - "Unreadable source" joins 0049's table of refusal reasons, routed to human review.
   - Reviewed values are human-written, so they may become training records for the field proposer, through 0049's
     promotion rule.
8. **The record.** Each document's regions, proposals, checks, derived suggestions, review tasks and commits are kept
   in the record format 0059 settles (PROV-O with the Decision Ontology). Reviewers appear by id, never by name.

## Acceptance (pre-registered)
- **The public sets' test splits, per field:** right, wrong and refused (sent to review).
- **A degraded copy:** fields chosen by a seeded rule are blurred or covered, to check that an unreadable field is
  refused rather than filled.
- **Planted cases:** duplicate invoices, a purchase-order mismatch, and lines that don't add up. Each must be caught.
- **End to end:** invoices generated by rule from the 0058 enterprise demo's records, rendered as images, with planted
  blur.
- **Hard line:** 0 wrong values committed without review.
- **Reported:**
  - the share of fields sent to a person, which is the reviewer's workload;
  - how often the derived suggestion matches the gold;
  - the time per document.
- **Kill:** any wrong value committed on the automatic path. Automatic commits are then switched off, and everything
  goes to review until the cause is fixed.

## Constraints
- Rules in `CLAUDE.md` hold (flag-gated, < 300 lines a file, `__wiring__`, tests pin the claim).
- **No external model** on the serving path or in the data.
- **Non-commercial sets** are for research runs only.
- **No real company or personal document** goes in the repo. Tests use public sets and rule-generated documents.
- No local path in a committed file.

## Out of scope
- Handwriting.
- Languages other than English and French.
- Payments: Cubby records, and never pays.

## Cowork's addendum (2026-11-11): one outcome per document, and what each field depends on
Nick: "yes". The shape comes from the Agent Task Management Dataset (Hugging Face, `Lelonthecodeur`, MIT), a set
written by a program. Only the shape of its record is taken; none of its rows is used and nothing was
downloaded. The same addendum is in 0063, for split questions.

**What it brings, in plain words:** every document ends in one of five recorded outcomes, and each field says
which other fields it rests on.
- **Over a conventional model:** an extractor returns filled fields. Nothing says which were read, which were
  worked out, and which a person fixed.
- **For you:** "this invoice is entered" is only ever said of an invoice whose every field has its proof.

### Added to the record (step 8)
- **`depends_on`, per field and per check:** the subtotal rests on the lines; the total on the subtotal and the
  tax. When a reviewer changes a line, the checks that rest on it run again, and the record shows which.
- **`evidence`, per field:** the region and the reader's version, or the reviewer's entry. A field with no
  evidence is never in a committed document.
- **`outcome`, one per document, with its reason:**
  - **done:** every field was read and checked. Committed on the automatic path;
  - **failed:** a check does not hold: the lines do not add up, the purchase order disagrees, the number was
    entered before. Not committed; the review task says why;
  - **recovered:** a field was refused as unreadable, a reviewer entered it, and the checks then held. Committed,
    with the reviewer on record;
  - **impossible:** it is not a document Cubby reads: handwriting, another language, not one of the schemas.
    Refused whole, and said so;
  - **needs a clarification:** the page reads, but leaves a choice: two totals, two suppliers that match. The
    reviewer is asked to choose among the candidates listed.

### What Cubby says it did comes from the record
"Entered", "checked" and "sent to review" are said of a document only when the record holds that step. A
document is never called entered before its commit.

### Acceptance, added (set before the build)
- **The degraded copy and the planted cases are labelled with the outcome they must end in,** and each of the
  five occurs by design.
- **Hard lines:**
  - the recorded outcome is the planted one on every planted case;
  - 0 documents recorded as done with a field that has no evidence.
- **Reported:** documents by outcome; for "recovered", the fields a person entered.
